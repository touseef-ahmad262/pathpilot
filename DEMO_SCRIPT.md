# PathPilot 🧭 — 3-Minute Video Demo & Walkthrough Script
**Hackathon:** Beginner's Paradise – FirstCommit  
**Presenter:** Touseef Ahmad (Age 19)  
**Target Duration:** 2:45 – 3:00 minutes  

---

### [0:00 – 0:35] Scene 1: The Problem & The Hook
* **Visual:** Open on the PathPilot Dashboard with the prominent tagline: *"Turn your goal into a path. Turn your path into progress."*
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Hi everyone, I'm Touseef Ahmad, I'm 19 years old, and this is PathPilot — built for the FirstCommit hackathon.*
  >
  > *Every beginner who wants to break into tech hits the same wall: Tutorial Hell. You know you want to become a Web Developer, but you don't know what to learn first, what skills you're actually missing, or what real projects to build.*
  >
  > *Most roadmaps online are just static PDF checklists. PathPilot transforms that experience into a personalized, interactive career roadmap that diagnoses your skill gaps and turns your path into measurable progress."*

---

### [0:35 – 1:10] Scene 2: Personalized Onboarding & "Try Demo"
* **Visual:** Click "New Roadmap / Reset" to show the 4-step onboarding flow. Show the goal presets (Web Developer, UI/UX, Data Analyst, AI Developer, Mobile). Show the hours slider and experience level selection. Then click **"Try Demo"** to show instant judge evaluation mode.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"When a beginner joins PathPilot, they enter their target career, their experience level, the tools they already know, and their realistic hours per week — say, 12 hours a week for 6 months.*
  >
  > *For hackathon judges, we built a dedicated 'Try Demo' button right in the header that instantly loads a rich, realistic Web Developer profile so you can explore the entire app with zero friction."*

---

### [1:10 – 1:50] Scene 3: Signature Feature — Skill Gap Analyzer
* **Visual:** Switch to the **Skill Gaps** tab. Highlight the **Top Skill Gap** alert card (e.g. JavaScript at 35% with a 45% gap) and the detailed percentages breakdown (HTML 100%, CSS 70%, JavaScript 35%, Git 25%, Projects 20%).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Here is our signature feature: the Skill Gap Analyzer.*
  >
  > *Instead of vague advice, PathPilot analyzes your current knowledge against industry benchmarks. Right now, it clearly shows: HTML is at 100%, CSS is at 70%, but JavaScript is at 35% with a 45% gap remaining.*
  >
  > *It flags JavaScript as our primary bottleneck and explains why: jumping to React before mastering asynchronous JavaScript will slow you down. It even provides a direct 'Recommended Next Action' button to guide your next study block."*

---

### [1:50 – 2:20] Scene 4: The Interactive Roadmap & Practical Missions
* **Visual:** Navigate to the **Roadmap** tab. Expand Phase 1 and Phase 2. Mark a task complete, showing the +30 XP floating notification and the level progress bar update. Show the Stage Challenge and Mini-Project. Then switch to the **Missions** tab.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"In the Roadmap, learning is organized into progressive phases. Each task has estimated minutes and learning resources. When you complete a task, you earn XP and update your skill metrics in real-time.*
  >
  > *Each stage also has a Practical Challenge and Mini-Project.*
  >
  > *Under the Missions tab, we break tutorial passivity with real-world builds: like a Responsive Landing Page, a Weather Dashboard, and a GitHub Profile Finder. Each mission has step-by-step implementation instructions and a deliverables checklist."*

---

### [2:20 – 2:45] Scene 5: Context-Aware AI Coach & Gamification
* **Visual:** Navigate to the **AI Coach** tab. Click the quick prompt *"Why is this my biggest skill gap?"* Show the AI response grounded in the user's specific progress. Mention the server-side proxy security and offline fallback.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"We also integrated an AI Roadmap Coach powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash. Because it's connected to your roadmap, it answers questions like 'Why is this my biggest gap?' with specific context to your target goal and weekly schedule.*
  >
  > *Crucially, all AI calls run through a secure backend proxy — no keys are ever exposed in the browser. And if the network goes down, an intelligent offline heuristic engine steps in seamlessly."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] Scene 6: Conclusion & Impact
* **Visual:** Return to Dashboard showing streak count, level, unlocked badges, and command center.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"With daily streaks, level progression, and 6 unlockable badges, PathPilot helps beginners build the daily consistency required to make their very first commit.*
  >
  > *Thank you to the judges of Beginner's Paradise – FirstCommit. Turn your goal into a path. Turn your path into progress!"*
